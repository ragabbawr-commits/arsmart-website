المواصفات التفصيلية

  • Support Both No Neutral & With Neutral Wire: The ZBM5 is
  • designed to adapt to your home’s wiring configuration. Whether
  • or not a neutral wire is available, it can be easily connected and
  • used.
  • Compatible with Multiple Zigbee Hubs: ZBM5 integrates with
  • Zigbee hubs, such as SONOFF NSPanel Pro, SONOFF iHost,
  • SONOFF ZB Bridge-P, SONOFF ZB Bridge-U, ZBDongle-E/P.
  • Works with Home Assistant: ZBM5 seamlessly integrates with
  • third-party open-source platforms, such as Home Assistant(
  • Zigbee Dongle is required, e.g. SONOFF ZBDongle-P
  • ZBDongle-E), making it perfect for your home automation needs.
  • Detach Relay Mode: With the detach relay enabled, smart
  • lights won’t lose power when the wall switch is turned off,
  • allowing them to remain smart at all times. *Supports connecting
  • smart lights only in the neutral required mode.
  • Fully Functional: ZBM5 has all the most basic functions, like
  • remote APP control, voice control, timing schedule, smart scene,
  • etc, making your life more convenient.
